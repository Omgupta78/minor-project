How to name the photos

One folder per student — easiest

CSE 3rd Year A/
  CS-001 Ravi Kumar/    front.jpg  left.jpg  right.jpg
  CS-002 Priya Singh/   1.jpg      2.jpg

The folder is named <roll> <name>. The photo filenames inside do not matter.

All photos in one folder

CS-001_Ravi_Kumar.jpg
CS-001_Ravi_Kumar_2.jpg
CS-002_Priya_Singh.jpg

Roll number, then _, then the name. A trailing _2 is a second photo, not part of the name.

A spreadsheet — most reliable

students.csv
roll_no,name,photo
CS-001,Ravi Kumar,photos/ravi-1.jpg
CS-001,Ravi Kumar,photos/ravi-2.jpg
CS-002,"Singh, Priya",photos/priya.jpg

Export it from Google Sheets as CSV and put it beside the photos. Filenames stop mattering, and names with commas or accents survive.

  • Roll numbers may contain hyphens (21-CSE-014). Only the first space or underscore separates roll from name.
  • JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP and iPhone HEIC are all read. Photos are re-saved as JPEG.
  • Three photos per student — front on, turned slightly left, slightly right — identifies noticeably more of the room than one.
  • A face must be at least {{ 80 }} px across, in focus and evenly lit, or that photo is rejected with a reason.
  • Up to {{ max_enrol_photos }} photos per student are kept.

Full details, including the Google Drive steps, are in IMPORT.md in the project folder.
